Private notes that land in your vault.

Write or dictate. Everything stays in this browser until you send it. Each entry becomes a plain markdown file with provenance frontmatter in your vault's inbox/ — exactly where memex expects new captures — and "Today's journal" keeps a daily/ note.

Connect a vault in — a folder, the local bridge, or just download the files.

Settings

Where entries go

A sent entry is written to <vault>/inbox/ (or the write_dir in your vault's governance.yml) with the Capture Anywhere frontmatter (title, status: inbox, captured_via, source_url) plus captured_at, sources, tags, and logged to .memexlab/log.jsonl. "Today's journal" writes daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md from the daily-note template.

This browser can't write to folders (Safari, Firefox, iOS). Use the bridge or downloads.

How to start the bridge 
curl -O https://memexlab.xyz/notes/bridge.py
python3 bridge.py --vault "~/path/to/your/vault" --reindex
# app + bridge at http://127.0.0.1:7357 — Safari and iPhone (same Wi-Fi, --host 0.0.0.0) included
